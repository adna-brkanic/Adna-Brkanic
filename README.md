## What I build

### 🧠 Internal AI knowledge assistant
**The problem:** Sales and CSM teams were hunting through SharePoint, the website, the blog, and internal docs for answers mid-conversation.

**What I built:** An AI assistant trained on 2,000+ pieces of content, from public materials (blogs, web pages, how-to articles) to internal sales decks, one-pagers, and battle cards. Reps ask in plain language and get a clear answer, organized by vertical, feature, and region, so they walk into every conversation as the most knowledgeable person in the room.

**The impact:**
- **500+ questions per week**, each one a search that used to take minutes
- **~40 hours saved per week** (estimated at 5 minutes per question)
- **A demand signal I can act on.** Frequent questions with no good answer become new blog and help content. Frequent questions about features we don't have become input for the product roadmap.

### 📄 Pricing proposal generator
**The problem:** Quoting a multi-product software bundle means knowing which products pair, what's discounted, and which rules apply.

**What I built:** A tool that encodes the company's sales logic (bundles, promotions, upgrades, seat changes, fee rules) so reps can produce a polished, customer-ready PDF proposal while still on the call.

**The impact:** Faster quotes, fewer pricing errors, and a professional leave-behind in the customer's hands before the call ends.

### 🎯 Custom sales deck builder
**The problem:** Every meeting needs a different story, but reps shouldn't be improvising with unapproved content.

**What I built:** A tool where reps assemble a deck for their specific meeting from approved slides. A few core slides are required, and the rest is their choice (vertical, feature, testimonial). Each deck comes with talking points, demo links, and supporting collateral.

**The impact:** Messaging stays consistent while reps tailor the conversation. On the back end, I can see which slides get used, how long each takes to present, and what copy resonates, so I know what to create more or less of.

### 🚀 Cross-functional GTM launch framework
**The problem:** Launches ran ad hoc, with every team working from a different playbook.

**What I built:** A company-wide launch process covering intake, prioritization, readiness, and visibility, so product, PMM, marketing, sales, training, and CSM all work from the same framework. It has C-suite approval and runs launches across every tier.

I've adapted this model for several organizations.

## How I work

- **Start with the buyer.** Interview sales, customers, and leadership before writing a word of messaging.
- **Build systems, not one-offs.** If I do something twice, it becomes a framework or a tool.
- **Close the loop with data.** Track what gets used and what resonates, then adjust.
- **Tell one story, tailor the angle.** The facts stay the same and the emphasis changes by audience.
